Delete your data

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Last updated: October 20, 2026 · App: EV Charge Tracker (package com.chartmann.evchargetracker) · Developer: Hartmann Studios

EV Charge keeps your data on your phone. Here is how to delete it, and what happens to purchases.

Delete inside the app

Open a log or a vehicle and choose Delete; deleting a vehicle removes its logs and reminders. Settings → Delete all data wipes everything.

Backup files

Backup files you saved to Google Drive or elsewhere are yours to delete in that app.

Delete everything

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → EV Charge → Storage → Clear storage. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing on our side to delete.

Pro subscription

The subscription is tied to your Google Play account, not to the app's data. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. After reinstalling, Settings → Restore purchases brings Pro back while the subscription is active.

Data held by Google

Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing is retained by Google under its own policies; you can manage ad settings in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads and at My Ad Center.

Need help?

open an issue on GitHub with "Privacy or data question" and we will help. Please do not post personal data in the issue.
