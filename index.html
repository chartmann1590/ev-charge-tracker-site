Free and Pro

The free app is the real app, with ads. EV Charge Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play; the price shows in the app in your currency.

Free

  • One vehicle with unlimited logs
  • Home vs public split, efficiency and battery health
  • Service reminders and live charging
  • Home-screen widget
  • Ads on list screens

Pro

  • No ads
  • Unlimited vehicles
  • Fleet overview: every car side by side
  • Backup file to Drive or any storage provider and restore on another phone

Private by design

Your vehicles and logs stay on your phone. No account, no server of ours. The optional backup file goes only where you save it.

In your language

On first launch you pick your language. The app downloads a free on-device translation pack from Google (ML Kit) for it and translates its screens on your phone; you can change the language any time in Settings. Translations are automatic, so a phrase may read a little differently than a native speaker would put it.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your phone.

Does it track my location?

No. The app does not ask for location. You choose "home" or "public" yourself when you log a charge.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Pro can save a backup file to Google Drive or any storage app through the Android file picker and restore it on another phone.

Does it work for gas or hybrid cars?

Yes. EV math is the focus, but fuel logs with price per gallon or litre are supported.

How do I get help?

Open an issue on our GitHub page.
